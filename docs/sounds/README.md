# Timebox Mini built-in sound previews

These are room recordings of a Timebox Mini played at device volume 15 and captured with a Logitech BRIO microphone. They are not copies of sound assets extracted from the device. The clips are trimmed to exclude the audible capture markers, and the raw Voice Memo is intentionally not published because it contains unrelated room ambience.

The 2026-08-16 capture was interrupted by a Bluetooth disconnection. Home Assistant history shows that the ESPHome proxy remained online while the Timebox link was offline from 13:21:36 to 13:29:28 Europe/Amsterdam, then briefly dropped again from 13:29:55 to 13:30:05. Only identifiable clips are included here.
